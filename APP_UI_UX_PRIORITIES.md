# Well Kept app UI/UX priorities

Updated 2026-09-13 from the [full interface review](./INTERFACE_POLISH_REVIEW.md). This ranking covers signed-in homeowner and cleaner screens, the invited-cleaner response, and checkout outcomes. Landing, login, sign-up, and the related verification/welcome screens are excluded. The findings came from source review.

**Implementation status:** Ranks 1–7 and 9–10 are implemented. Rank 8 is intentionally unchanged so the radar continues to show live activity. TypeScript and the production build pass; a visual pass through authenticated screens with test data remains useful.

Impact ranks the effect on a user's ability to understand and complete a task. “Visual change” describes what a person would notice after the **recommended, smallest coherent fix**, not every possible implementation.

| Rank | Screen and issue | Recommended change | UI/UX impact | Visible change you would notice |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Post a job:** “Add photos” shows a selected count, but photos are neither submitted nor saved. [Form](./components/marketplace/simple-job-request-form.tsx#L294), [post route](./app/customer/jobs/create/route.ts#L28). | **Remove the photo option until attachments are supported.** If photos are a near-term product requirement, implement upload, saved previews, and error handling instead. | **Very high.** A homeowner can believe cleaners received visual context that was silently discarded. | **Obvious:** the dashed “Add photos” control disappears from the notes section. An implemented upload would instead add thumbnails, removal controls, and upload feedback. |
| **2** | **Homeowner conversation:** the “Send message” composer opens the device SMS app with a draft; it does not send within Well Kept. [Composer](./components/marketplace/message-composer.tsx#L9). | Keep the current SMS handoff but label the action **“Open SMS”** and add one short line saying the message is sent in the phone's Messages app. Reserve “Send” for real in-app messaging. | **Very high.** This is the main communication action, and its current result differs from the interface's promise. | **Clear:** the send icon/label and helper copy change. The chat area can remain visually the same. Building in-app messaging would be a much larger product change. |
| **3** | **Homeowner job carousel and cleaner job feed:** multiple jobs are indicated by dots/counts, but visible previous/next controls are absent. [Homeowner CSS](./app/customer/homeowner.css#L1014), [cleaner deck](./components/marketplace/nearby-job-swipe-carousel.tsx#L140). | Add restrained, 44px previous/next controls only when there is more than one job; keep swipe and keyboard navigation. | **High.** It makes the next job discoverable and gives every input method a direct path. | **Obvious when multiple jobs exist:** small chevrons or labelled controls appear near the carousel. Single-job screens stay unchanged. |
| **4** | **Homeowner account and provider-selection drawer:** 36px stepper buttons and 32px shared default drawer buttons are small for touch. [Stepper CSS](./app/customer/homeowner.css#L3565), [drawer](./components/marketplace/provider-selection-drawer.tsx#L113). | Increase the effective targets to at least 44×44px. Where space is tight, enlarge the hit area while retaining the visible shape. | **High on mobile.** This reduces mistaps in home details and the provider-confirmation flow. | **Slight to moderate:** steppers may grow by 8px and drawer buttons by roughly 12px, or the visual size can stay nearly identical with larger invisible hit boxes. |
| **5** | **Homeowner conversation:** the ellipsis “Conversation options” button has no action. [Thread header](./app/customer/messages/%5BbidId%5D/page.tsx#L54). | Remove the button until it has a real menu, and re-center the header content. | **Medium-high.** It removes an interaction that currently feels broken. | **Clear but small:** the ellipsis disappears and the conversation header becomes simpler. |
| **6** | **Homeowner conversation:** every provider is shown as “Responds quickly” without measured response data. [Thread header](./app/customer/messages/%5BbidId%5D/page.tsx#L52). | Remove the claim or replace it with verified provider information. | **Medium.** Trust cues matter when choosing a cleaner; an unsupported cue can set the wrong expectation. | **Small:** that one-line status under the cleaner name changes or disappears. |
| **7** | **Both role apps:** routine navigation slides the whole page and bounces the active-nav highlight. [Role shell](./components/marketplace/role-swipe-shell.tsx#L66). | Use a brief opacity/state cue for routine tabs; retain directional movement only where it explains a real spatial transition. | **Medium.** Frequent navigation becomes calmer and more consistent with `DESIGN.md`. | **Noticeable in motion only:** pages stop sliding on each tab change; still screenshots look essentially the same. |
| **8** | **Homeowner “Notifying Cleaners” and cleaner “Searching for jobs”:** multiple radar and status animations loop indefinitely. [Homeowner CSS](./app/globals.css#L2605), [cleaner CSS](./app/cleaner/provider.css#L1067). | Play a brief entrance or animate on actual status change, then hold a static state. Keep reduced-motion handling. | **Medium.** Long waits demand less attention without hiding status. | **Noticeable over time:** the radar settles instead of pulsing/sweeping continuously; layout, copy, and colors remain. |
| **9** | **Homeowner messages:** search and conversation-header buttons are 43px, 1px below the app's touch target rule. [CSS](./app/customer/homeowner.css#L1173). | Bring effective targets to 44px. | **Low.** This closes a small consistency gap. | **Nearly invisible:** a 1px box adjustment. |
| **10** | **Homeowner multi-job position:** “1 of 3” uses proportional digits. [Workspace](./components/marketplace/homeowner-jobs-workspace.tsx#L98). | Apply tabular numerals to the position label. | **Low.** Prevents a tiny horizontal shift as the selected job changes. | **Not visible in a still image:** the count stays steadier while swiping. |

The first two are **expectation and trust fixes**. Ranks 3–6 change specific screen elements. Ranks 7–10 are refinement. The current press scale of 0.975, established radii/shadows, and existing tabular price styling should stay as defined in `DESIGN.md`.
